# Glossary

Shared words for talking about ChristOnline. One row per term, one sentence of meaning. When a doc or schema needs the idea, it uses this word, unchanged.

Definitions marked **to confirm** need Daniel's wording before any feature brief uses them.

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Member | A person on the church roll who regularly attends and may hold other roles. | to confirm |
| Visitor | A person attending who is not yet a member; tracked so they can be welcomed and followed up. | to confirm |
| Check-in | Recording that a person is present at a service or event; the attendance app's core act. | to confirm |
| Welcome (flow) | The series of steps that greets a newcomer and connects them to people and follow-up. | to confirm |
| Warden | Church overseer with authority over access and privacy decisions in the attendance model. | to confirm |
| Attendance | The record that a person attended a specific service or event. | to confirm |
| CrossCheck | Prior product: community knowledge hub with recommendations and trust-weighted voting. | established |
| Church Welcoming App | Prior product: live Sunday check-in and welcoming flows. | established |
| Developer | Founder building the platform; platform operations across orgs, without member-data access. | to confirm |
| Admin | Church staff with full access within their one church. | to confirm |
| Manager | Head of a ministry team; manages day-to-day operations and Helpers within that ministry. | to confirm |
| Helper | Volunteer on a ministry team; sees the minimum person details needed for the task. | to confirm |
| Dev board | The in-app development task board at `/dev/board`, available on localhost. | established |
